Future development
====================

This study has relied on rather simplistic approximations, and several details have been omitted due to a lack of available data. 
In particular, when working with urban planning materials, it is crucial to take budget considerations into account. 
Furthermore, some areas of the city were excluded from the analysis because of insufficient data. No restrictions on construction 
sites were assumed; however, in the real world, this is an unrealistic scenario. Building on roads is not straightforward, 
and expropriating private property is not easily feasible without encountering significant bureaucratic obstacles.

Additional analyses could also be conducted on social conditions, connections to bus stops, and climate data—factors that were not 
available during the development of this project.

Furthermore, we plan to expand the GUI functionality to support real-time computation of new results based on 
incoming data and to accommodate multiple data formats.
